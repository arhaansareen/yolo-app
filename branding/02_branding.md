# Yolo — Branding

## Name
**Yolo**
Note: "Yolo" has existing trademarks in adjacent spaces (Apple's YOLO computer vision mark, a YOLO social/messaging app trademark, YOLO Adventures for travel/recreation apps, plus several defunct/discontinued Yolo apps). Fine for building/prototyping — get an IP/trademark consult before a real public launch or App Store filing.

## Logo
**Direction:** Black background, all-lowercase gold cursive script reading "yolo." Elegant/luxury feel — like a members-only invite rather than a loud consumer app icon. No icons, no borders, no extra graphics — just the gold script centered on black.
